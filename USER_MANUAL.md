## 1. Getting Started & Sign In

When you first launch Filer Flow, you have three quick ways to access the application:

* **Explore as Guest:** Tap **"Explore Calculator as Guest"** to immediately calculate income taxes or try out features without creating an account.
* **Sign Up / Login with Email:** Enter your email address and a secure password. You can request a password reset email anytime if you forget your credentials.
* **One-Tap Google Sign-In:** Tap the **Google** button to link your account securely and enable seamless cloud backups.

---

## 2. Navigating the Home Dashboard

The dashboard provides a visual snapshot of your financial standing.

### The Spending Donut Chart
* **The Colored Rings:** Visually displays the proportion of your money that has been **Spent** versus what remains as your **Net Balance**.
* **Center Summary:** The center of the circle shows your actual **Net Balance** in PKR.

### Metric Chips (Below the Donut)
* 🟢 **Net Surplus / Balance:** Shows your unspent balance and percentage saved.
* 🔴 **Spent:** Shows your total expenditure and spending percentage for the selected period.

### Filtering by Fiscal Year & Calendar Month
At the top of the dashboard, you have two dropdown filters:
* **Financial Year Filter:** Tap to switch between Pakistani tax years (e.g., *FY 2024–2025*, *FY 2023–2024*, or *All Years*).
* **Month Filter:** Tap to focus on a single calendar month (e.g., *July*, *August*, *December*) or choose *All Months* to inspect the entire year.

## 3. Recording Incomes & Expenses

Filer Flow organizes your money across a structured, 3-level category system.

### Manually Adding a Transaction:
1. Tap the green **`+`** button on the dashboard or transactions screen.
2. Select the transaction type:
   * **Income:** Salary, business revenue, freelance payouts, rental profit, dividends.
   * **Expense:** Groceries, utilities, dining out, transport, medical, fees.
3. Enter the **Amount** in PKR.
4. Pick the **Date** of the transaction.
5. Select the **Category** and **Subcategory** (e.g., `Living Expenses > Utilities > Electricity`).
6. *(Optional)* Add a short note or merchant name for your personal reference.
7. Tap **Save Transaction**.

---

## 4. Using the AI Receipt & Bill Scanner

Instead of typing numbers manually, you can snap a photo of any printed bill, invoice, or store receipt:

1. Tap the **Camera / Scan Receipt** button when adding an expense.
2. Align the camera with the receipt and take a clear, well-lit photo.
3. The on-device AI scanner reads the receipt and extracts:
   * **Merchant / Store Name**
   * **Transaction Date**
   * **Total Amount Paid**
4. Review the auto-filled fields on the screen, adjust any details if needed, and tap **Save**.

> **Note:** The receipt scanner runs 100% on your device—your photos are never uploaded to any public web server.

## 5. Calculating Your FBR Income Tax

Estimate your exact Pakistani income tax liability according to official Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) tax slabs:

1. Open the **Tax Calculator** from the bottom navigation or side menu.
2. Select your **Tax Year** (supports all fiscal years from *2015-16* up to *2026-27*).
3. Select your **Taxpayer Category**:
   * **Salaried Individual:** Select this if salary constitutes 75% or more of your annual income.
   * **Business / Non-Salaried Individual:** Select this if you are a sole proprietor, freelancer, doctor, contractor, or shopkeeper.
   * **Association of Persons (AOP):** For registered business partnerships.
4. Type your income into either the **Monthly Income** or **Annual Income** field (the app automatically converts between monthly and annual values).
5. The calculator instantly displays:
   * **Monthly Tax Deduction**
   * **Total Annual Income Tax**
   * **Effective Tax Rate (%)**
   * **High-Income Surcharge** (if annual earnings exceed statutory thresholds)
   * **Applicable Slab Breakdown** (showing the base tax and marginal percentage applied)

## 6. Claiming Tax Deductions & Credits

If you have already paid withholding tax on everyday utilities and assets, you can adjust these amounts directly against your annual tax:

1. In the Tax Calculator, open the **Deductions** section.
2. Enter the withholding taxes already deducted on your behalf:
   * 📱 **Mobile Phone Bills / Prepaid Cards (Section 236):** Tax charged on mobile recharges.
   * ⚡ **Electricity Bills (Section 235):** Withholding tax billed by utility providers (LESCO, K-Electric, IESCO, etc.).
   * 🌐 **Internet & Broadband Services:** Advance tax collected on internet packages.
   * 🚗 **Motor Vehicle Token Tax:** Advance tax paid during annual car registration or token renewal.
3. The app subtracts these tax deductions from your total payable amount to show your **Net Tax Payable**.

## 7. FBR Live Verification (ATL, NTN, CPR)

Verify official tax documents in real-time without leaving the app:

* **Active Taxpayer List (ATL) Status:**
  * Enter your **13-digit CNIC** (e.g., `35201-1234567-1`).
  * Tap **Check ATL Status** to verify whether you or a vendor are listed as an Active Filer with FBR (granting lower withholding tax rates).
* **NTN Verification:**
  * Search by National Tax Number or CNIC to check company and individual business registrations.
* **CPR Payment Verification:**
  * Enter your **Computerized Payment Receipt (CPR)** number to confirm that taxes deposited at the bank or State Bank have been credited to the FBR treasury.

## 8. Generating & Sharing PDF Statements

Generate clean, professional statements for your accountant, tax consultant, or visa applications:

1. Go to the **Transactions** tab.
2. Tap the **Export / PDF** icon at the top right.
3. Choose the reporting period (e.g., *Current Month*, *Specific Fiscal Year*, or *Custom Range*).
4. Tap **Generate PDF**.
5. You can preview the document immediately, print it via wireless printers, or share it as a PDF via WhatsApp, Email, or Drive.

## 9. Security, App Lock & Cloud Backup

### Biometric App Lock
Protect your financial privacy from anyone holding your phone:
1. Open **Profile > Security Settings**.
2. Switch on **Enable Biometric Lock**.
3. Choose **Fingerprint** or **Face Unlock**.
4. The app will lock automatically whenever you switch apps or minimize Filer Flow.

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
1. In **Profile > Security Settings**, select **Two-Factor Authentication**.
2. Scan the provided QR code with **Google Authenticator**, **Microsoft Authenticator**, or **Authy**.
3. Enter the 6-digit confirmation code to activate. Every login will now require your authenticator code.

### Encrypted Google Drive Backup
* Never lose your records if you switch phones.
* Go to **Settings > Google Drive Backup** and tap **Backup Now**.
* Your data is saved directly to your personal Google Drive account in a private app data folder. No third-party servers ever touch or store your financial figures.
* On a new device, tap **Restore** to download and restore all your past transactions.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: Do I need an internet connection to use Filer Flow?
**A:** No. All expense tracking, receipt calculations, and tax slab computations work completely offline. Internet is only required for live FBR verification tools and Google Drive cloud backups.

#### Q: Is my financial data shared with the FBR?
**A:** No. Filer Flow is a private personal helper. Your income, expenses, and receipts are stored locally on your device and inside your private Google Drive backup. Nothing is submitted to the FBR unless you personally export your figures and file through the official IRIS portal.

#### Q: Can I edit or delete transactions later?
**A:** Yes. Simply tap any transaction in the list to update its amount, date, or category, or swipe to delete it.
